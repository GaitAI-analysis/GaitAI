## Description
GaitAI MobilityCare turns a 10–20 second walking video into movement insight you can track over time.

WalkScan
• Record a walk with the camera or choose a video from your library
• The analysis runs on this device; the video does not leave your phone unless you choose to keep or share it
• Free: cadence (steps per minute) and step-time balance
• Pro: step-time variability, stride regularity, trunk sway, left/right step timing, the contact timeline, a quality report and trends against your previous scans

RehabTrack
• Compare a new scan with your baseline, metric by metric, in the units they were measured in

History and reports
• Every scan is kept on your phone; share a report through the standard share sheet

GaitAI Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly). Prices are shown in the app in your local currency. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase; the subscription renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

All values are two-dimensional estimates from a single camera. They are movement indicators, not a diagnosis. Discuss health concerns with a clinician.
