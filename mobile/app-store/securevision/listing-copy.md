## Description
GaitAI SecureVision reads short videos of spaces you are authorised to analyse and turns them into counts and zone activity. The analysis runs on your device.

CrowdSense
• Upload a video; an on-device person detector counts who it can see, frame by frame
• Free: current, average and peak count
• Pro: density timeline, per-frame counts, detection review and export

Zone & occupancy
• Draw a zone on a frame of the same video
• Entries, exits and dwell are estimated from frame-to-frame tracking of detections whose foot point falls in the zone
• Free: total entries and current occupancy; Pro: the full timeline and report

Activity and reports
• Every analysis is kept on your phone; share a report through the standard share sheet
