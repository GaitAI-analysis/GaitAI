# GaitAI SecureVision — App Review notes (draft)

## What the reviewer needs
No account or sign-in exists. Everything works offline except the subscription sheet (StoreKit).

## How to exercise the app
1. Open the app → onboarding → Home → **Start CrowdSense**.
2. Wait for "Movement engine ready" (a few seconds on first launch).
3. **Upload a video** of a space with people in it (any short clip; a stock pedestrian clip is fine).
4. The person detector runs on the device and opens the result: current, average and peak
   count are free; the density timeline and per-frame review are Pro.
5. From Analyze → **Zone & occupancy**: pick the same video, drag the polygon handles on the
   frame, name the zone, **Analyse** → entries, exits and occupancy.
6. **Unlock full analysis** opens the paywall with the App Store prices. Restore purchases and
   Manage subscription are on the same screen.

## Subscriptions
Auto-renewing: `securevision_pro_monthly`, `securevision_pro_yearly` in one group ("GaitAI Pro").

## Content and privacy
The detector produces bounding boxes only. The app does not identify, recognise or store
anything about individuals, and tells users to analyse only footage they are authorised to use.

## Demo clip
A CC0 stock pedestrian clip can be linked in the notes for reviewers; do not use CCTV footage
of real premises.
