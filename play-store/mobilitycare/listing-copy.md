## Full description (4,000 characters max)

GaitAI MobilityCare brings GaitAI's movement intelligence to clinical and
care settings: gait and mobility understanding from ordinary video and
wearable signals, presented as outputs a clinician can review.

What MobilityCare covers

- Gait assessment: walking speed, cadence, step and stride patterns, symmetry
  and balance indicators, read from a short walking video.
- Rehabilitation tracking: objective movement change across a recovery,
  session by session, so progress is visible rather than assumed.
- Mobility trends over time: longitudinal views that surface decline or
  improvement early.
- Fall-risk and mobility insight: indicators that support a clinician's
  judgement; they do not replace it.
- Sports and performance movement analysis, elderly-care monitoring and
  wearable (WatchCare) signals, as modules on the same platform.

Built for clinician review

Every output is structured to be read, questioned and exported: reports,
trends and indicators rather than a black-box score. MobilityCare supports
assessment and monitoring workflows; it is not a diagnostic device and does
not make clinical decisions.

Privacy by design

GaitAI can understand movement without seeing a person's full identity,
using pose, silhouettes, trajectories or wearable signals, and keeps only the
information the task needs.

One platform, twelve modules

MobilityCare is one of two GaitAI product families. Its twelve modules share
the GaitAI Core engine with GaitAI SecureVision, the privacy-aware safety
family, which is a separate app.

This app opens the MobilityCare experience at gaitai.in. An internet
connection is required.
