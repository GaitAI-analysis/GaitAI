## Full description (4,000 characters max)

GaitAI SecureVision applies GaitAI's movement intelligence to safety and
spatial understanding: how people move through a space, read in a
privacy-aware way and presented to operators as reviewable indicators.

What SecureVision covers

- Crowd understanding: movement and density indicators across concourses,
  campuses, venues and public spaces.
- Movement and anomaly analysis: candidate movement-event indicators that an
  operator reviews, rather than automatic decisions.
- Public-space safety: passenger-flow, evacuation and queue insight for
  transport hubs, events, retail and smart-city environments.
- Industrial and worker safety: fall and slip indicators, restricted-zone
  entry and fatigue-like movement trends on factory and warehouse floors.
- Authorised post-event investigation support and access-related movement
  analysis, as modules on the same platform.

Privacy-aware by default

SecureVision is designed around privacy-first architecture, lawful
deployment and auditability. It works from pose, silhouettes and
trajectories rather than a person's full identity, and keeps only what the
task needs. PrivacyGuard defines the default posture.

One platform, twelve modules

SecureVision is one of two GaitAI product families. Its twelve modules share
the GaitAI Core engine with GaitAI MobilityCare, the clinical and mobility
family, which is a separate app.

This app opens the SecureVision experience at gaitai.in. An internet
connection is required.
